# Institutions

<p class="lede">An institution is a group of people who have agreed to keep minutes. The world has a great many of them, and the minutes are where most of my evidence comes from, which is to say that my evidence is very thorough about biscuits.</p>

I have grouped them as they group themselves, which is by what they would most like to be asked about. Every one of them was written to for this volume, and every one replied, and two of the replies were in one word.
