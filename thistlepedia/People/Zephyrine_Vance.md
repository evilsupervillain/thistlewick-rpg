<p class="lede"><b>Zephyrine Vance</b> is a hedge mage who has lived in <a href="../Places/Thistlewick">Thistlewick</a> since 1989, when she left the <a href="../Institutions/The_Collegium_Arcanum">Collegium Arcanum</a> after an incident involving the Dean, a duck, and what the enquiry called, twice, "an unsanctioned quantity of fire". The Dean is fine. The duck is fine. She would like it understood that everyone is fine.</p>

When Bram came to her table in the <a href="../Places/The_Gilded_Turnip">Gilded Turnip</a> and began to speak, she said, "Don't. Whatever it is, don't." He said it was a quest. She asked what sort. He said a tower, with a Dark Lord in it. "That's the most fire-adjacent thing anyone has said to me in eleven years," she said. "Give me my hat."

## The incident

The facts are these. In 1989, at a dinner at the Collegium, Miss Vance said aloud that a junior fellow's paper had gone out under somebody senior's name. She was told to let it go. She did not let it go. Some time later in the evening there was fire, in a quantity that had not been sanctioned, in the vicinity of the Dean and a duck. Nobody was hurt. The enquiry used the phrase twice.

I have read the enquiry. I have also read the paper: *On the Kindling of Damp Materials*, by Dr Hapgood, which went out under the name of the Master, and the Master is a committee. The quantity of fire was unsanctioned. The point she was making was correct. Both of those have been true since 1989, and for eleven years only one of them was in the file.

She was the best student of her year. Hedge magic is the discipline of doing the same work with worse equipment in somebody's garden, and she has been very good at it for a long time. It was not what she trained for, and she drank at the Gilded Turnip in the manner of somebody waiting for a letter.

## The letter

It came. It bore the Collegium's crest, and it informed her that she was on study leave. "STUDY leave. I was EXPELLED. There was an ENQUIRY. It used the phrase TWICE." It turned out that the papers expelling her had been drafted and initialled and never filed, and she had therefore been on unpaid study leave for eleven years, in good standing, and owed eleven years of library fines. The fines were the largest sum of money anyone on the Quest ever saw written down. Sub-Librarian Prewitt was courteous, immovable and correct.

How the matter ended depends on whom you ask, and I have asked her, and the answer I got was a look. The Collegium's register says she is a member of the Collegium in good standing, with her fines paid on the Library desk out of a purse. I have also seen a letter, in her hand, filing her own expulsion, eleven years after the event, at her own request. I have printed both, because one of them is true, and she has asked me to leave it that way.

## The duck

The duck survived the incident, and the Collegium, not knowing what else to do with a bird that had given evidence at a formal enquiry, made it an Honorary Fellow. It has since sat in Congregation, held a chair, and acquired views on the endowment. In 2000 a vote was called and the quorum was short by one duck, which was on a pond four miles away. It had to be walked. It has four hit points and walks into everything. The party walked it.

The Collegium has been quorate ever since. The duck's vote is held by proxy, permanently, by Zephyrine Vance. She has not yet used it. She is saving it.

## Other fires

- **The Cinder Fen.** Nine hundred acres of sodden fen had been drowning the road to Great Dunnage since the parish's last fire person retired in 1970. The remedy is fire, and in 2000 she was asked for it, in writing, with a form that asked her to state the quantity. "It's the first piece of paper about me and fire that I've liked." The fen was burnt, sanctioned, in triplicate; the road is dry; the form came back countersigned.
- **Dean Fenwold.** The Dean has made a second career of being the man who was fine, and does an after-dinner talk about it. He asked her to do the fire again, small, in a hall, with buckets. She refused, then agreed, on terms. It went wrong in exactly the way it had gone wrong the first time, for exactly the same reason, which is that the room was wrong. His talk now ends with the room being wrong, and Dr Hapgood's paper has Dr Hapgood's name on it. I take that to have been the terms.
- **The lay-by.** The hedge mages have an association. It meets in a lay-by on the west road, because none of them will host, and has been trying to agree a name for nine years. One of them taught himself out of a book with four pages missing, and is therefore better than the Collegium at exactly four things. It has four members now, and still no name. Item one is carried forward.

## Afterwards

She is still in Thistlewick, now on purpose. She no longer drinks at the Gilded Turnip like somebody waiting for a letter, because the letter came. She still has the duck's vote, and still has not used it. I asked her what she was saving it for and she said she would know it when she saw it, and that it would not be soon, and that I was not to put that in. I have put it in. She is welcome to set fire to the page.

<div class="marginalia">Middle C on the Gilded Turnip's piano is a little warm. She tells me that is her, and has been since 1989.</div>
