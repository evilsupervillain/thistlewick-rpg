<p class="lede">The <b>Forty-Eighth Company</b> is what I have called the people who went north with <a href="Bram_Thistle">Bram Thistle</a> in the year 2000, because they have never called themselves anything, and an encyclopedia needs somewhere to put them. The clause allows a Chosen One three companions. Bram kept to it, never taking more than three at once, and in the course of the Quest he took ten different people, which the clause does not forbid, because nobody on the Committee had thought of it.</p>

Seven of the eleven were found in Thistlewick, without leaving it. Three were found in the Wyvern, forty miles south, where no Chosen One before him had thought to go, and one was found in the north, holding a form.

## Which three

Which three were with him at the top is a matter on which I have taken evidence from everybody, and the evidence is that it was all of them. I have not ruled on it. I set out the claims in <a href="../History/The_Obligatory_Quest#the-company">The Obligatory Quest</a>, and I will add only one thing. Roland Fairweather is the only member who does not claim it, because he left at the door. He came back after the ending, and was not called away the second time; see <a href="Roland_Fairweather">his article</a>.

<div class="marginalia">I asked each of them separately who had been the best company on the road, meaning to print the totals. Eight of them said Bram. Bram said Mrs Thistle's pies, which were not on the road, and Spare said Spare. Nix asked me what the question was worth. I have not printed the totals. - H.B.</div>

## Afterwards

The company did not disband, because it was never formed in the first place. Most of its members went home, and home was mostly in Thistlewick, so they still see each other every day and nod. Spare went back to Upper Clanging in the spring for the tenth Cotterill. When the party went down the <a href="../Places/The_Stair">Stair</a> after the ending, it was the same company: three at a time, as the clause requires, even though the clause is no longer binding. Bram says he is used to it now.
