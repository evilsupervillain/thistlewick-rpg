<p class="lede"><b>Perpetua Small</b> woke up on the beach at <a href="../Places/Nether_Sopping">Nether Sopping</a> in 1994 with no memory, a headache, an excellent posture and an expensive tiara she does not appear to have noticed she is wearing. She has lived at the <a href="../Places/The_Slain_Wyvern">Slain Wyvern</a> since. Everybody there has been very kind to her. Nobody will tell her anything. It has been eight years, and they want to see her get there on her own.</p>

## A note on this article

This article has been written so that it can be left on the bar. The Wyvern asked me to do that, by a show of hands, and Dorcas Thrupp read it before it went to the printer, and took one sentence out, and gave it back. I have not put the sentence back. If you know the thing that this article does not say, the Wyvern would be grateful if you looked very hard at the ceiling.

## What she knows

> "Perpetua Small. I think. I woke up on the beach six years ago with no memory and a headache. Everyone here has been very kind. Nobody will tell me anything, but they've been very kind."

She can ride. She can fence. She speaks four languages and does not know why. She folds every napkin she is given into a little crown, without looking, and has no idea why, and is extremely good at it. And when she walks into a room, people stand up.

> "They stand up! For me!"

When she told the Forty-Eighth this, the entire tavern behind her was looking very hard at the ceiling, and I am told that he considered telling her, and that the room, as one, silently begged him not to, and that he did not. I think it is to his great credit. It would have been very easy.

Every month a bottle of very good wine arrives at the Wyvern from a palace somewhere, addressed TO WHOM IT MAY CONCERN, and is put beside her plate, and she drinks one glass of it, on Sundays, and says that it is very good, and that somebody must have a lot of wine.

## Her theory

> "It'll come back to me. Probably something dull. Dairy, I expect."

She has given this a great deal of thought, and has refined it. "Cheesemaking. It would explain the four languages." She means that a cheesemaker has to trade abroad. I asked how it would explain the fencing. She said that I had clearly never tried to cut a really old cheddar.

## Gerald

There was a Gerald. I have been told by Dorcas Thrupp that he proposed to Miss Small every Sunday for a year, in the Wyvern, on one knee, at lunch, and that the Wyvern handled it, and that that is all that is being said about Gerald. I have found that it is.

<div class="marginalia">When I gave Miss Small the draft of this article to read, she read it all the way through at the bar, slowly, and then she said that it was very fair, and that she liked the bit about the cheddar, and that she couldn't help feeling there was something in it she was missing. The whole room looked at the ceiling. She said, "There! You're all doing it again," and laughed, and folded the proof into a crown. I have it on my desk. It is a very good crown. - H.B.</div>

## Afterwards

She is still at the Wyvern, and has not got there yet. In the summer of 2001 she remembered a word, out of nowhere, over breakfast, which was *precedence*, and she has decided that it is a kind of cheese. The bottle still comes every month. The room still stands up. She says it is a lovely town for manners.
