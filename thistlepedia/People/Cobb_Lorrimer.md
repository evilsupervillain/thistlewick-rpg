<p class="lede"><b>Cobb Lorrimer</b> is a thief of <a href="../Places/Nether_Sopping">Nether Sopping</a>, registered with the Guild under T, who steals things and says so, and wears a hood about it, and has never in his life been ashamed of anything except one signature. He plays the Dark Lord for the children on the front, in the afternoons, and loses.</p>

## The contest

He leant on the Guild's wall in the year 2000 with his arms folded, the way a man leans who would like to be asked what he does, and when <a href="Nix">Nix</a> came by he told her. He found her whole vocabulary an insult. "LOCATES. AHEAD OF SCHEDULE. THE CLIENT. It's stealing with a waistcoat on. It's a job you've failed to apply for."

She said that nothing in her shop was stolen, and that she could tell him how she had got every piece of it. He said, prove it, and produced a list, in a child's hand, from the two children on the front who were playing Chosen One with no kit: ONE ROPE. ONE HELMIT. ONE SWORD. ONE DARK LORD (WHO LOSES). First back with the lot. A client, a list and an afternoon. No fighting.

He was back before dinner with a rope off the ferry, a helmet out of the outfitters' window, and the very good sword off the knees of a man who was having a day off, and who came up the street after it in his socks. <a href="Sops">Sops</a> and <a href="Tibb">Tibb</a> sat on a barrel each, as judges. "Where'd you get them?" "I STOLE them. I'm a thief. It's the whole point of me." Tibb said that his mother said they were not to have stolen things, and said it twice.

Nix laid out a coil of rope, a pot and an oar, each with a receipt on top: lent until sundown, and signed for. The ferry, the outfitters and the man in his socks took their things back off Mr Lorrimer, one at a time, politely, and he watched them go. "You can't win a THIEVING contest on PAPERWORK." She said it had not been a thieving contest. It had been a list.

## The fourth item

There was one more thing on the list, and nobody in Nether Sopping would be it, and you cannot steal a Dark Lord. Nix took out a form and a pen and offered him the post: Dark Lord, afternoons. Villainous. A proper criminal position. He would be good at it.

"...Villainous?"

He signed. He read what he had signed, (WHO LOSES), a moment too late, and the children were already running at him with an oar and a pot on. He lost magnificently, three times before sundown, and stormed off up the street in a huff, and left his tools on the barrel, which meant they were located. Nix wrote him a receipt.

## Afterwards

He has come back every afternoon since, under protest. "Don't. I signed it. I read it after. It's afternoons." He is getting very good at losing. The children say that he has a speech now, before the end, and that it is very long and very frightening and has a bit at the end where he says he will return, and then he does, the next afternoon, which they consider the best part.

He has asked Nix for a receipt for his tools at every meeting since, and she has given him one at every meeting, and he has kept them all, and he would like it known that he has not used a single one of them, because it would be admitting something.

He is still registered under T. Registrar Pell has asked him whether he would like an entry under D as well, and he said no, and then asked what the form was called.
