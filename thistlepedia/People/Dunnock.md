<p class="lede"><b>Dunnock</b> is a hedge mage of the west road, and a member of the association of hedge mages that meets in an igloo in a lay-by and has not got a name. He is self-taught, from a book with four pages missing, which he worked out for himself, and did better, as it turns out; he is better than the whole <a href="../Institutions/The_Collegium_Arcanum">Collegium Arcanum</a> at exactly four things. Somebody in the igloo says <i>Collegium</i> in the voice people use for weather, and it is generally him.</p>

## The book

He was born in 1952 in the lock-keeper's cottage on the west road, the lock-keeper's son, and was a lock-keeper himself for thirty years. At twelve he found a book of magic in a hedge, a proper one, bound, with every page in it but four. He taught himself from it, at the lock, in the evenings, and when he came to the four missing pages he worked out for himself what must have been on them, from what came before and after, and did them.

> "Self-taught, me. The book I learned from had four pages missing, so I worked those four out for myself. Did them better, as it turns out. I'm better than the whole Collegium at exactly four things."

The four are pages nineteen, twenty-three, sixty and a hundred and four, and he will tell you what three of them do. Page twenty-three is *Kindle*, which you do not aim, but sort of mean, at everything, and it sorts itself out.

## The igloo

The association has met every winter for eleven years, in an igloo at the end of a lay-by on the west road, which Mrs Quickset made the first winter, because none of them would host, and it is nobody's house, so nobody has to do the biscuits. It has not melted in all that time, and it is the one thing the association has ever agreed on. Dunnock would like that corrected. "We didn't agree on it. She just did it."

Item one on the agenda, every year, is the name. It has been carried forward for eleven years.

## Zephyrine

In the year 2000 Zephyrine Vance, who was expelled from the Collegium Arcanum for an unsanctioned quantity of fire, asked to join. There was one objection. It was withdrawn after Dunnock showed her page twenty-three, which is not in the book, because it is missing, and which he has done better. She said that that was not how fire works. A gorse bush, a milestone and the lay-by's litter bin caught fire all at once, neatly, and went out, with nobody anywhere near them. She said that it should not have worked. "The page was missing," said Dunnock.

There is a chair at the folding table now with nobody's coat on it. She proposed a name. It was a good name. It was carried forward.

<div class="marginalia">I asked him what the Collegium could do that he could not. He thought about it for some time, over his pipe, and then said that the Collegium could find all of its pages, and that he had always felt sorry for it. - H.B.</div>

## Afterwards

He is forty-nine. The association met again in the winters of 2000 and 2001, in the igloo, which has still not melted, and Miss Vance attended both, and sat in the chair with nobody's coat on it, and proposed two more names, both very good, and both carried forward. Dunnock says that the association will have a name the year it needs one, and that a name is a page, and that some pages are better missing, because then you have to work them out.
