<p class="lede"><b>Sops</b> is the potboy at the <a href="../Places/The_Slain_Wyvern">Slain Wyvern</a> in Nether Sopping, and can tell a Chosen One by the standing, and will tell the Chosen One so. He also picks the flowers for the bedrooms, mostly dandelions, takes the trays up, and plays Chosen One on the front with Tibb, under rules that were, when I first heard them, well ahead of anything the <a href="../Institutions/The_Prophecy_Committee">Prophecy Committee</a> had managed.</p>

## The standing

Sops went to work at the Wyvern at nine, in 1997, for Mrs Thrupp, collecting the pots, and he was at once found to be very good at it, and also at noticing things, which Mrs Thrupp says is the same skill, applied to people. The thing he noticed first, and still notices, is the standing.

> "Are you a Chosen One? You look like a Chosen One. It's the standing. Like you're waiting for someone to tell you where to go. All of 'em do it. Mrs Thrupp says it's the prophecy posture and it ruins the chairs."

The Forty-Eighth was told this on his first visit, and then, on his second, "You're doing the standing again," and he says that he had not known he was doing it the first time and has not been able to stop knowing since. I have watched Sops pick out a Chosen One across a crowded bar at forty paces and be right. I have also watched him pick out a man who had come in about a drain, and be told so, and say that he was standing like one, and that he should think about it.

## The Wyvern

Sops picks flowers for the bedrooms every morning, on the way in, off the bank at the end of the front, and he arranges them in a jam jar on every washstand with great seriousness, standing back to look. They are mostly dandelions. Mrs Thrupp says that no guest has ever complained, and that two have asked to take them home.

He reports on the guests to anybody who will listen, and is a reliable reporter of every fact and of no conclusion whatever. "The Forty-Second and his wife are in. They come every year for the anniversary. They're SIXTY." Mrs Thrupp said good for them. He asked good for what, and got a bun. Of room four: "Room four's still not been down. I've stopped taking the tray up. I leave it and go."

<div class="marginalia">Mrs Thrupp has asked me to print that Sops is a very good potboy and the best she has had, and that is all that is being said. I have printed it. I have also printed nothing else, which I think is what she meant. - H.B.</div>

## The game

On the front in the afternoons Sops and Tibb play Chosen One. The rules are that one of them walks north and the other has to be the Dark Lord and lose. Neither of them wanted to be the Dark Lord. "I was the Dark Lord LAST time. You be the Dark Lord." They settled it by both being the Chosen One, and agreeing that the Dark Lord had stayed at home. "The Dark Lord's stayed home again. He does that now." I put it to the Committee, in a letter, that two children on the front at Nether Sopping had solved the Prophecy a year before the Forty-Eighth did. The Committee minuted the letter. I have the minute. It says *Noted.*

In the year 2000 <a href="Nix">Nix</a> and Cobb Lorrimer held a contest on the front, which Lorrimer believed was a thieving contest and Nix knew was a list, and the list had been written by Sops and Tibb. Lorrimer came back with a helmet and a sword, stolen. "Where'd you get them?" said Sops. Nix came back with a rope, a pot and an oar, each with a receipt on it. "...This one's got PAPERWORK," said Sops, which is still, two years later, the highest praise anybody in Nether Sopping can give.

There was one more thing on the list: ONE DARK LORD (WHO LOSES). Neither child would be it. Nobody in the town would be it. In the afternoons now, Lorrimer comes down the street in a teal hood, under protest, and loses.

## Afterwards

Sops is thirteen, and still potboy, and still picks the flowers, and has begun, this spring, to pick out the ones that are not dandelions as well, which Mrs Thrupp says is either growing up or ambition in him, and that she will let me know. The game on the front goes on in the afternoons. He is still the Chosen One. Lorrimer is still the Dark Lord, and still loses, and has got, Sops says, a lot better at it, though he says it kindly.
