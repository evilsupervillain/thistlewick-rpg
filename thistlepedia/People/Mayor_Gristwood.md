<p class="lede"><b>Josiah Gristwood</b> is the Mayor of <a href="../Places/Great_Dunnage">Great Dunnage</a>, a town of nine works, in the most hat you have ever seen. For five years he practised the preamble for the formal Opening of the Counting House, which was sealed in 1788 when the key was lost, and which is to be opened by a licensed person, when there is one, and there has never been one. The preamble takes forty minutes. He got it down to thirty-seven and a half. In the year 2000 the Door was opened informally while he was still on *mindful*.</p>

## The preamble

He was born in 1949 over the Chain Works, the foreman's son, and went into the Corporation's offices at sixteen, and rose, by being reliable, to Town Clerk's deputy, alderman, and in 1995 Mayor, the forty-first since Alderman Josiah Dunnage, who sold nails, and after whom he was named, by a mother with ambitions. On the evening of his election he was handed the preamble to the Opening of the Counting House, in the Corporation's book, as every Mayor has been since 1788, and told that it would be his duty to read it, in full, at the Opening.

> "Gristwood. Mayor. Forgive me. I'm practising the preamble, for the Opening. The Counting House! Sealed in 1788, when the key was lost. We've run the town on a memorandum."

> "It is to be opened formally, by a licensed person, with the preamble read in full. When there is a licensed person. The licence is a form. Nobody has ever filled it in. But the preamble is ready. It takes forty minutes. I'm down to thirty-eight."

He was the first Mayor in two hundred years to practise it. The others had read it once, in their first week, and put the book back. He says that he could not see the point of being Mayor if you were not ready, and that he did not know when the licensed person would come, and that it would be a very poor show to be found at the Opening forty minutes long.

## Mindful

In the year 2000 he asked the Forty-Eighth's company whether any of them was, by any chance, a licensed person, and was told no, and began the preamble anyway, while it was there. *AND WHEREAS the Corporation of Great Dunnage, being in Council assembled, and mindful -* Nix put an ear to the lock and did something small with two fingers, and it took nine seconds, and the Door stood open while he was still on *mindful*.

That was not the Opening. The Counting House is opened informally; the Opening proper will follow on completion of Form L. Behind the Door there was one room, one chair, and on the chair a note: WE SPENT IT. J. POCKLINGTON, CLERK TO THE CORPORATION. 1788. He sat down on the chair, as the first Mayor to do so, and read the preamble to it, in full, twice.

<div class="marginalia">The money stacked under the note came to one thousand seven hundred and eighty-eight crowns, which nobody can explain, and the Mayor said that it was not the Corporation's, because the memorandum says they have enough. He has put it in the Bonded Warehouse, under Mr Tibbs, as Bond No. 1788, *money, a little, provenance none whatever*, and the Corporation pays the rent on it. He says that it is the first sound financial decision the Corporation has made since 1788, and that it is a very good chair. - H.B.</div>

## The scissors

The same week, the Mayor lost his ceremonial scissors, on the Tuesday, and had to open two things with a knife, which he felt very keenly. They turned up on the paddle of the bottom lock on the Dunnage cut, in the culvert, where a boy from Upper Clanging found them, along with the paddle, and thirty of the Iron Company's boats went down the cut, and the Mayor cut the ribbon again, properly, with scissors.

## Afterwards

He is fifty-three, and still Mayor, and still in the hat. He has been composing a shorter preamble. It is, as of this spring, eleven minutes, and he is very proud of it, and says that it loses nothing of substance, and that the Corporation in Council assembled and mindful is all still there, only quicker. Form L is still blank. He keeps a copy in the hat, in case the licensed person should turn up while he is out.
