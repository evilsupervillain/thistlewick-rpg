<p class="lede"><b>Miss Privet</b> takes the minutes of the association of hedge mages, which meets once a year in an igloo at the end of a lay-by on the west road and has not got a name. She is a tall woman in a wide white hat, which she does not take off in the igloo, because there is room, and she has minuted every meeting the association has ever held, and every name anybody has ever proposed for it, all of them carried forward. She is the only member who will say what the association is called. She says it is not called anything, and that that is item one.</p>

## The minutes

A member of the public who looks into the igloo is welcomed, and told where he is, as far as that is possible:

> "Oh! A member of the public. Do come in. This is the ninth annual general meeting of the..."

and Mrs Quickset says "Of the association", and <a href="Dunnock">Dunnock</a> says "Of the association of hedge mages", and Miss Privet says, "Which is not its name." Then she invites the member of the public to propose one, strictly for the minutes. The Forty-Eighth was asked, and proposed nothing, and was minuted: *The member of the public abstains. Noted.* Others have proposed *The Hedge Mages*, and been told that it was too obvious, and that people would know what they were, and Miss Privet has written it down, under nine years of other things carried forward.

She was born in 1938, in a lane off the west road, and taught by an aunt, out of no book at all, in the evenings, the way hedge magic is taught in her family, which is by somebody saying "not like that" until it is. She can do several things. The one she uses most, and the one the association depends on, is a small charm that stops ink freezing, without which there would be no minutes, because it is an igloo. She says that it is not much of a spell, and that she would put it up against anything the <a href="../Institutions/The_Collegium_Arcanum">Collegium</a> has, for the purpose.

I pointed out to her that the three founding members are a privet, a quickset and a dunnock, which are two hedges and a bird that lives in one. She said that she had noticed, and that it had been raised, in the second year, and minuted as a coincidence, and that the minutes were correct.

## It's Vance

In the year 2000 the Forty-Eighth's party came to the igloo with <a href="Zephyrine_Vance">Zephyrine Vance</a> among them, and Miss Privet looked up, and put her pen down.

> "Oh. It's Vance."

It is the only time, in eleven years of minutes, that she has put her pen down during a meeting, and there is a gap in the minutes where it happened, about two lines long, which she has left. Everybody on the west road had heard of Miss Vance, who set fire to the Dean. Mrs Quickset said so. Dunnock said "Collegium." Miss Vance said "Formerly." Then Miss Privet picked her pen up again, and took item two, which in nine years there had never been, the admission of a member, and asked for objections, and there was one, and it went outside to look at page twenty-three.

A gorse bush, a milestone and the lay-by's litter bin caught fire at once, neatly, and went out. Miss Privet minuted it as *a demonstration*, and then: *Objection withdrawn. Carried. Item one?* Miss Vance proposed a name. It was a good name, Miss Privet says, one of the best, and it was carried forward with the others.

<div class="marginalia">I asked Miss Privet how many names had been carried forward, and she counted them for me, from the minutes, with her finger: sixty-one, three of them Miss Vance's. She then asked whether the member of the public would care to propose one, strictly for the minutes, and I did, and she wrote it down, and it is in the minutes now, sixty-two, *proposed by the member of the public (history)*, carried forward. I will not print it. It was not as good as Miss Vance's. - H.B.</div>

## Afterwards

The association has four members now, and no name, and item one has been carried forward at the tenth meeting and the eleventh. There is a chair at the folding table with nobody's coat on it, and Miss Vance sits in it, and Miss Privet greets her the same way every year: "Vance. Sit down, dear. Item one, carried forward." The igloo has not melted. Mrs Quickset made it, and it is still the one thing the association has ever agreed on, and Dunnock still says that they did not agree on it, and Miss Privet still minutes him saying so.
