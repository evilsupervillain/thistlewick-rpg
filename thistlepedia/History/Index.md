# History

<p class="lede">The history of the world, so far as anyone has written it down, is very largely the history of one document. Everything before the <a href="The_Prophecy_of_Thistlewick">Prophecy of Thistlewick</a> belongs to the elves, who will tell you about it if you ask and who have never been asked. Everything since belongs to the Prophecy, which has been asked a great deal and has answered, every time, in clauses.</p>

This volume holds the articles that are about *time* rather than about a person or a place.

| Article | What it covers |
|---|---|

| <a href="The_Chosen_Ones">The Chosen Ones</a> | All forty-eight of them, so far as they are known, with what became of each. |
| <a href="The_Obligatory_Quest">The Obligatory Quest</a> | The quest of the Forty-Eighth, from the bed to the throne room, and after. |
| <a href="Two_Reckonings">Two Reckonings</a> | Why the Tower is two thousand years old and four thousand eight hundred, and both are true. |
| <a href="Timeline">Timeline</a> | Every date I could pin, in order. |
