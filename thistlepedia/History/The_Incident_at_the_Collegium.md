<p class="lede">The <b>Incident</b> took place at the <a href="../Institutions/The_Collegium_Arcanum">Collegium Arcanum</a> in 1989, when a student called Zephyrine Vance, the best of her year, produced what the subsequent enquiry called, twice, "an unsanctioned quantity of fire", in a very small room, in the vicinity of the Dean and a duck. Nobody was hurt. She left. The Dean has been fine ever since, professionally. The duck was elected a Fellow, and the Collegium did not manage to hold a quorate meeting for the next eleven years, and nobody noticed why until the year 2000.</p>

## High Table

The facts, so far as I can establish them, are these. At dinner at High Table in 1989, Miss Vance said aloud that a paper, *On the Kindling of Damp Materials*, by Dr Hapgood, a junior fellow, had gone out under the name of the Master, and the Master is a committee. She was told to let it go. She did not let it go. Some time later in the evening, in a smaller room, the discussion was continued with the Dean, and there was fire.

> "An incident. A very gifted young woman, a very small room, and rather more fire than the room had been told about. The eyebrows grew back. Magnificently, I'm told."

Why there was a duck in the room is not in the enquiry's report, which says only that it was present, and that it gave evidence. I have asked three Fellows. Each of them said that it had always been there, and then looked as if they had never thought about it before, and did not want to start.

## The enquiry

The enquiry sat for a week and found that the fire had been unsanctioned in quantity, and said so twice, and found nothing about the paper, because nobody had asked it to. Miss Vance's expulsion was drafted, initialled by the Dean, and referred to the Registrar, and the Registrar did not file it, and the Collegium did not notice, because a student who leaves is referred, and a referral is not a departure. She went to <a href="../Places/Thistlewick">Thistlewick</a> and became a hedge mage and drank at the Gilded Turnip like somebody waiting for a letter. In the spring before she went she had borrowed three books from the Library, at a penny a day.

The Dean's portrait was painted that year. The painter was asked to be accurate about the eyebrows, and then, on reflection, to be a little less accurate.

## The duck

The duck had survived the incident, and the Collegium, not knowing what else to do with a bird that had given evidence at a formal enquiry, elected it an Honorary Fellow, and gave it a chair at the end of High Table with a cushion on it, and it went home to its pond, four miles off, and did not come back.

The Collegium has twelve Fellows who are entitled to sit in Congregation, and needs twelve for a quorum. Eleven of them come to things. The twelfth is a duck. Every meeting of Congregation from 1989 to 2000 was inquorate, by one, and everything that came before it was referred: four hundred and six items, including the Library roof, and every one of them with the word REFERRED against it in the minutes, in a hand that got more resigned as the years went on. Nobody worked out why. I have been through the minutes, and I can tell you exactly why, and I want to say in fairness that it took me two days, with the answer already in my hand.

## The year 2000

Three things were put right in 2000, each of them by Miss Vance, and none of them in the way the Collegium would have chosen.

**The letter.** Her pigeonhole was full. The letter in it informed her that she was on study leave, and had been for eleven years, in good standing, and owed eleven years of library fines on three books. How that ended depends on whom you ask, and both ends are in her article.

**The duck.** She read the notice of an inquorate meeting, eleven present, twelve required, and looked at it for some time, and then at the Lodge, and then at the road, and said, "We are going to fetch a duck." The duck saw her, and stopped paddling, and came out of the pond, both of them having been at the enquiry. It was walked four miles, into everything, and set on its cushion, which it fitted exactly. Congregation was quorate for the first time in eleven years. Dr Hapgood was elected to a Senior Fellowship, five for, five against, the chair abstaining, and the Honorary Fellow walking into the Dean's water jug and saying, once, quite clearly, *quack*. Items two to four hundred and six were carried by half past six. The duck named its proxy by walking into Miss Vance and staying there: PROXY - Z. VANCE. PERMANENTLY. She has not used it yet.

**The room.** The Dean asked her to do it again, small, in Hall, with buckets, for his talk. She agreed, on terms. It went wrong in exactly the way it had gone wrong in 1989, for exactly the same reason, which is that the room was wrong. The Dean did not move. He had not moved in 1989 either.

> "She said the room was wrong, you know. At the time. I remember thinking she was very probably right."

Dr Hapgood's paper has Dr Hapgood's name on it now.

## The talk

Dean Fenwold has given an after-dinner talk on the incident, *The Man Who Was Fine*, forty-one times before the year 2000 and a good many since. They laugh in all the right places. The line about the room being wrong was in it all forty-one times, and it was never there on purpose until the forty-second. It is the end of the talk now.

<div class="marginalia">The Dean has had the Hall measured since, and the measurements are before the room committee, which will consider them. I asked whether the Collegium had considered measuring the very small room. He said that it had not, and that nobody had been in it since 1989, and that he was not sure, now that I mentioned it, that anybody knew where it was. - H.B.</div>
