<p class="lede">The <b>Chosen Ones</b> are the forty-eight people whom the village of <a class="new" href="../Places/Thistlewick">Thistlewick</a> sent north, under the <a href="The_Prophecy_of_Thistlewick">Prophecy</a>, to put down the Dark Lord in the <a class="new" href="../Places/The_Obligatory_Tower">Obligatory Tower</a>. It worked forty-seven times. Thistlewick will tell you so. Ask it what happened the other times, and it will tell you that it worked forty-seven times.</p>

"Everyone tells you it's an honour," the father of the Forty-Seventh told the Forty-Eighth, on the morning he left. "Nobody tells you it's a rota."

## How a Chosen One is chosen

By the <a class="new" href="../Institutions/The_Prophecy_Committee">Prophecy Committee</a>, in session, at a moment it finds convenient (see [Two Reckonings](Two_Reckonings)). The candidate does not need to be present, and the last one was not: the minutes of the meeting at which <a class="new" href="../People/Bram_Thistle">Bram Thistle</a> was chosen list, under *apologies for absence*, his name and one goat. He was asleep. The goat has not been found.

The Chosen One is then summoned to <a class="new" href="../Places/Prophecy_Hall">Prophecy Hall</a>, where Elder <a class="new" href="../People/Elder_Wispel">Wispel</a> unrolls the Prophecy until it reaches the far wall and gives the charge: take companions, up to three; go north; kill the Dark Lord; come home. "In that order, ideally." The Chosen One receives a copy of the Prophecy in triplicate, five hundred crowns and three potions from the parish fund, and a sword. Every one of them did the sword.

What happened next was, for forty-seven of them, the same. They went north. They climbed the Tower. They found the Dark Lord, <a class="new" href="../People/Grimspite_the_Inevitable">Grimspite the Inevitable</a>, reading, and he put his bookmark in and said, "So, you have come at last," which he meant the first time. They fought him, and they won, and before they reached the stairs clause twelve renewed him. They came home and Thistlewick said that it had worked.

"Forty-seven of us," <a class="new" href="../People/Halbert_Quy">Halbert Quy</a> told the Forty-Eighth. "We all did the sword. Not one of us ever asked the man why he doesn't stop."

## What became of them

They came home, and they were awkward. A Chosen One who has come back knows what is at the top of the Tower, and says so at meetings, and asks why the Committee is already drafting the next one. Somewhere about the Ninth, Thistlewick wrote [clause nineteen](The_Prophecy_of_Thistlewick#clause-nineteen-the-resettlement-clause), and from then on a returning hero received a pension, a cottage, and such distance from the village as was mutually agreeable, which was forty miles, to <a class="new" href="../Places/Nether_Sopping">Nether Sopping</a>. That is where they are, those of them who are anywhere. Most of them drink in the <a class="new" href="../Places/The_Slain_Wyvern">Slain Wyvern</a>. Thistlewick does not write.

## The forty-eight

The Committee keeps a souvenir book, *The Forty-Seven*, with a portrait, a date and a pressed flower on every page. The flowers are all turnip flowers. I have seen it, across a table, and was not permitted to copy the dates, which are Committee property. So I have printed below only what I could get from somebody who was there, and where nobody was, I have left the space.

**Forty-seven, or forty-six.** Grimspite says he said "so, you have come at last" forty-seven times and was renewed forty-seven times, and the Obelisk in his tower lists forty-seven RENEWED. The Forty-Sixth says he got as far as the woods. Miss <a class="new" href="../People/Ott_Hoyle">Hoyle</a> of Upper Clanging, who counts for a living, counts forty-six that went up that road. I have all three numbers, and I have not been able to make them into one. I set them down here side by side, which is what an encyclopedia is for.

</div>

## Monuments

**The Wall of the Forty-Seven**, in Prophecy Hall: forty-seven portraits. Forty-six of them are looking at the viewer. One of them is looking at the door. I have asked which, and why, and was told that it was a very old portrait. At the end of them there is now a forty-eighth frame, new and empty, with a card pinned in it: PORTRAIT TO FOLLOW. SITTER AT THE COAST. The sitter is not at the coast. He is at home, two fields away, and has been for two years. Nobody has changed the card. A second card, the Quills', is tucked into the corner to make room.

**The Obelisk**, in the Obligatory Tower: forty-seven entries, each a date and a name and the word RENEWED. There is room for a forty-eighth. It will not be needed.

**The memorial at Nether Sopping**: a hundred and eighty-one names. It lists everybody who went north with them - healers, hired swords, cousins, a cook, somebody listed only as "the lad who carried the rope" - and not only the Chosen Ones, which is the difference between Nether Sopping and Thistlewick in one stone.

## The end of the list

When the Forty-Eighth had read the Prophecy through and it had stopped being binding, Grimspite signed him a receipt and sent him home with a message. "Grow your turnips. There isn't a forty-nine. Say that to them, when you get in. Say: there isn't a forty-nine."

He said it. The Committee minuted it. It has not, so far as I can tell, been believed, but it has been true for two years, which is the longest anything has been true in Prophecy Hall.
