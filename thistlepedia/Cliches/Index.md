# A Glossary of Cliches

<p class="lede">An adventuring convention is a thing that happens on every quest because it happened on the first one, and nobody has thought to stop it. The Forty-Eighth walked into very nearly all of them. This volume lists them, with where he walked into each, and who was still annoyed about it.</p>

<p class="hatnote">The Prophecy's own account of the Quest keeps a tally of the conventions a Chosen One walks into on the way, and reads the total back to him at the end. I have not seen the number in the Forty-Eighth's account; I have seen the words, which are <i>very nearly all of them</i>, and the reason given, which is that he read the notice board, searched the barrels, opened things that were obviously not chests, and went south, which nobody does. By my own count there are a little over two hundred to be walked into between Thistlewick and the top, and more since. What follows is a selection, alphabetical, and each headword is marked as a convention the way I have marked them in my notes, with a dotted line, so that the reader can see where one has been walked into. - H.B.</p>

## A

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: Prophecy Hall">**The abridged edition.**</span> A work too long to read, made shorter for the reader's convenience. *The Prophecy of Thistlewick (Abridged)* is on Elder Wispel's desk in <a href="../Places/Prophecy_Hall">Prophecy Hall</a>, and is exactly as long as the unabridged one, the abridging having been done by the Committee.

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: Firstfield Meadow">**The ambush.**</span> See *The tutorial*, and Norbert, who is contracted to lose.

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: the Slain Wyvern">**The amnesiac.**</span> A young person found on a beach with no memory, an excellent posture and something expensive on their head, whom everybody is very kind to and nobody will tell anything. <a href="../People/Perpetua_Small">Perpetua Small</a> woke up on the beach at <a href="../Places/Nether_Sopping">Nether Sopping</a> six years before the Quest, in a tiara she has not noticed. The whole of the <a href="../Places/The_Slain_Wyvern">Slain Wyvern</a> looks very hard at the ceiling. Still annoyed: nobody; that is rather the point.


## B

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: every barrel he saw">**Barrels.**</span> A barrel is searched. It is the law. The one at <a href="../Places/Firstfield_Meadow">Firstfield</a> says WELL DONE. YOU HAVE SEARCHED A BARREL. THERE WILL BE MANY MORE. There were. The Prophecy's account names this as one of the four reasons for its verdict.

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: the Gloamwood">**The beanstalk.**</span> Goes straight up into the canopy of the <a href="../Places/The_Gloamwood">Gloamwood</a>, and stops, as though it had thought better of it. A card at the bottom: GIANT NOT IN. PLEASE DO NOT CLIMB. THIS MEANS YOU, JACK.

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: the Gilded Turnip; the Safety Valve; the Eight Hours">**The bed that mends everything.**</span> In stories, heroes lie down on any bed they find and wake up feeling much better. At the <a href="../Places/The_Gilded_Turnip">Gilded Turnip</a> it is twenty crowns, and Prudence would like you to know that it is still twenty crowns. At <a href="../Places/Restwell">Restwell</a> it actually works, which is the whole of that town.

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: Nix's Emporium">**The blue potion.**</span> Is mana. Everybody knows that, and nobody knows how. The one in <a href="../Places/Nixs_Emporium">Nix's Emporium</a> says so, with the small print OR IT IS BLUE WATER. ONE OF THOSE.

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: the Guild; the Slain Wyvern">**The bounty board.**</span> A cork board of monsters with prices on, behind a rope eleven inches high. At the <a href="../Institutions/The_Adventurers_Guild">Adventurers' Guild</a>, members only. The Crab of Unusual Size and <a href="../People/Meredith_Crooke">M. Crooke</a>, both discharged.


## C

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: the Obligatory Tower">**The cell.**</span> FOR CHOSEN ONES WHO HAVE BEEN CAPTURED. In the <a href="../Places/The_Obligatory_Tower">Obligatory Tower</a>. In four thousand eight hundred years nobody has ever been captured. It seemed rude.

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: the Gloamwood">**The chest that is not a chest.**</span> Alone, in the middle of a wood, on a path, in perfect condition. Everybody knows. Nearly everybody opens it. Miss Halloway's entry is under <a href="../Bestiary/The_Road_North#chest-mimic">chest mimic</a>. Still annoyed: the mimic, which is splinters.

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: Thistlewick, asleep">**The Chosen One.**</span> Selected by prophecy from among the humble, to go north. The Forty-Eighth was chosen by the <a href="../Institutions/The_Prophecy_Committee">Prophecy Committee</a> while he was asleep, and was therefore unable to object, and pointed out several things when he woke up, none of which helped. Still annoyed: nobody he will name.

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: the Obligatory Tower">**The coffins.**</span> A dark tower ought to have coffins, for the look of it. Every coffin in the row in the Obligatory Tower says RESERVED.

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: the Obligatory Tower">**The convenient cupboard.**</span> Just before the end, a cupboard of exactly what the party needs. In the Tower there are ten hooks with brass labels, CHOSEN ONE, HEALER, SMITH and so on, and two added later in a cheaper brass, RIVAL and CATALOGUER, and a tenth for GUEST, with a note in pencil. Somebody has been keeping the list up to date for four thousand years, which is unnerving.

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: wherever he was">**The courier who finds you.**</span> The post reaches a hero anywhere, in any weather, in any cave. A man in a red coat and a flat cap with a satchel found the Forty-Eighth's company in a good many towns, on behalf of Spare Cotterill's correspondence, and once, at the edge of the lamplight, a long way down <a href="../Places/The_Stair">the Stair</a>. *Post?*


## D

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: the Sable Hart; everywhere Corvin was">**The doomed rival.**</span> Dark, brooding, carries a black sword, and is fated to be overshadowed. <a href="../People/Corvin_Ash">Corvin Ash</a> held the post. At <a href="../Places/The_Sable_Hart">the Sable Hart</a> five other doomed rivals keep a league table and are very fair about seating. Still annoyed: the league table, which stops at fourth.

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: Thistlewick">**The dungeon rumour.**</span> A child runs up to you to say that there is a DUNGEON! To the NORTH! You have heard. You will hear again.


## E

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: Prophecy Hall">**The elder who gives the quest.**</span> Unrolls a scroll. It reaches the far wall. Says that it has worked forty-seven times, and, asked about the other times, that it has worked forty-seven times. See <a href="../People/Elder_Wispel">Elder Wispel</a>.


## F

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: Firstfield Meadow">**The fetch quest.**</span> Somebody's sheep are lost and must be found. At Firstfield the sheep are pigs, and are not lost, and have been asked to lose themselves a bit. If you decline, you are told *But thou must*, and then told sorry, the Guild gave her that line.

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: the Fair">**The fortune teller.**</span> NEVER WRONG. TWO CROWNS. <a href="../People/Mother_Selvidge">Mother Selvidge</a>, at the <a href="../Places/Wayside_Sights#the-fair">Fair</a>, a long way from anywhere, the way fairs always are. I have not printed her reading, because it was not mine.

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: Weeping Bottom; Much Pottering; Ashcombe">**The founder's stone.**</span> A stone, a date, a name and a reason, with a joke cut into it underneath by a later hand. Weeping Bottom's says the sea air is the finest cure for the chest, in the opinion of every physician consulted, and somebody has added, *None of whom had been*.


## H

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: Much Pottering">**The haystack.**</span> Searched, out of habit, for a needle. NO NEEDLE. THIS IS MUCH POTTERING.

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: the west road">**The hermit.**</span> <a href="../People/Edwin_Tarrant">A man on a rock</a>, looking at the middle distance with great authority, who says *You seek wisdom*, and then gives you his shopping list. He has been up there nineteen years and would like some cheese.

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: Mrs Emberthwaite's lair">**The hoard.**</span> A dragon's, of gold, which a hero may take a coin from. It is dusted every morning with a feather. You could take a coin. Mrs Emberthwaite looks at you over her spectacles. You could not.


## K

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: the Obligatory Tower; the Guild">**The key under the mat.**</span> The door of the most dangerous building in the world has a mat in front of it that says WELCOME, and under the mat there is a key labelled SPARE - DO NOT LOSE AGAIN. Nix would not use it: the bar was on the inside. The Adventurers' Guild's form was also under its mat. Still annoyed: Mr Pell, who is aware of the tower, and does not find the pattern amusing.


## L

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: Penultima">**The last shop before the end.**</span> Priced accordingly. <a href="../Places/Penultima">Penultima</a> has two, and the actual last one is shut. In the window of the other is the <a href="../Artefacts/The_Sword_of_Ultimate_Ending">Sword of Ultimate Ending</a>, which sir cannot afford to hold.

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: the Obligatory Tower; the Forbidden Archive">**The library of forbidden books.**</span> In the Tower: *Villainy for Beginners*, read once, carefully, with a bookmark in chapter one, and *The Monologue: A Handbook*, whose chapter one is called *Don't*. In the Forbidden Archive at <a href="../Places/Olde_Evermore">Olde Evermore</a>: a book stamped FORBIDDEN in four languages, which is biscuit recipes.

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: the Obligatory Tower">**The lockers.**</span> The key is never in the lockers. A card in the first one says so.


## M

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: the Slain Wyvern">**The mysterious advisor.**</span> Kindly, old, and watched from week three, because in every story the kindly old advisor turns out to be the villain. <a href="../People/Councillor_Dree">Councillor Dree</a> was watched for eleven weeks and then tied up, and was not the villain, and sits alone in the Slain Wyvern now, deliberately, with great composure. Still annoyed: Councillor Dree, very politely.


## N

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: Thistlewick; Nether Sopping; Fairholme; and others">**The notice board.**</span> Read in every village, because it is the law. Thistlewick's has LOST: ONE GOAT. ANSWERS TO COUNCILLOR, and WANTED: CHOSEN ONE. POSITION FILLED. The Prophecy's account names reading it as one of the four reasons for its verdict.


## O

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: Thistlewick; Firstfield">**The old man who gives you something.**</span> *It's dangerous to go alone. Take this.* Old Hollis at Firstfield has given eleven thousand, two hundred and six sticks. Thistlewick's own old man gave the Forty-Eighth something else, which was: *Everyone tells you it's an honour. Nobody tells you it's a rota. Come back, lad.* His boy was the Forty-Seventh.


## P

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: the Obligatory Tower; Hithermost; and a good many kitchens">**The pots.**</span> Looked in, or under, in case. In the Tower, a note: I WOULDN'T EITHER. - G. At <a href="../Places/Hithermost">Hithermost</a>: STILL NO. At the Gilded Turnip, a pot labelled POT, and inside it a label, INSIDE OF POT.

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: Rosamund's tower, Fairholme">**The princess in the tower.**</span> Waiting to be rescued. Princess Rosamund of <a href="../Places/Fairholme">Fairholme</a> has been rescued a great many times, and has written a memoir, *Rescued Again*, now in its eleventh volume, in which every chapter is named for a rescuer, and every one of them is written about very kindly.

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: everywhere, and in particular the top of the Tower">**The prophecy.**</span> Ancient, binding and vague. Ours was precise. See <a href="../History/The_Prophecy_of_Thistlewick">The Prophecy of Thistlewick</a>, and Councillor Fenn, who said it was a subscription.


## R

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: the summit of the Obligatory Tower">**The real last boss.**</span> The villain falls, and then the thing behind the villain rises. At the top of the Tower, after Grimspite, the Prophecy unrolled itself to enforce clause twelve. It was the real last boss. It lost. See <a href="../History/The_Obligatory_Quest">The Obligatory Quest</a>.

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: the coast; the Gloamwood">**The retired hero.**</span> Has a sword over the fireplace, exactly like the one that was over yours, with dust on the upper edge, which takes a long time and a great deal of not looking. <a href="../People/Halbert_Quy">Halbert Quy</a>, the Forty-Fifth. Also the Forty-Sixth, on his log, who decided that the woods were enough.


## S

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: Hithermost; Firstfield">**The save point.**</span> A book, or a crystal, at which progress may be kept. The one at Hithermost is a Book, and is Sir Dunstable's name all the way down the page. Firstfield reminds the visitor that he may save at any time, which he always could.

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: Gladhollow">**The secret passage.**</span> One volume of the minutes sticks out further than the rest. You pull it. Something goes clunk.

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: Thistlewick, Bram's house; Number Forty-Five's">**The sword over the fireplace.**</span> Kept for exactly this occasion. It came off the wall of Bram Thistle's house with a sound that can only be described as smug. See <a href="../Artefacts/">Arms and Artefacts</a>.


## T

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: Wick & Barrow's">**The ten-foot pole.**</span> Every adventurer needs one. It does not say what for. Nobody has ever said what for. Everybody buys one. Wick & Barrow's keep them round the back, because they would not fit on the shelf.

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: the Gloamwood">**The thing in the woods.**</span> Which everybody fears, and nobody has ever got close enough to describe. It was nine snakes, courting. See <a href="../Bestiary/The_Road_North#the-thing-in-the-woods">the Bestiary</a>, which is very firm about it.

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: Penultima; Hithermost; Frostwick; Sump; and others">**The town sign.**</span> Names the town, so that you know where you are. Penultima's says THE LAST TOWN BEFORE THE END, and under it LAST CHANCE FOR POTIONS, and under that (WE MEAN IT). Hithermost's says that it is the last village before the mountain, and, added later, AND, FOR SOME, THE FIRST ONE AFTER IT.

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: Firstfield Meadow">**The tutorial.**</span> Explains walking to somebody who has just walked. Forty-seven Chosen Ones skipped it, by pressing the button really fast. The Forty-Eighth did not, because nobody had told him you could. See <a href="../Places/Firstfield_Meadow">Firstfield Meadow</a> and <a href="../Institutions/The_Guild_of_Guides">the Guild of Guides</a>.


## V

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: Great Dunnage">**The village that has never heard of you.**</span> The watchman at Great Dunnage had never heard of the Forty-Eighth. He has since found him in the ledger, for me: BRAM (THISTLEWICK) - PROPHECY, SOME SORT.


## W

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: Number Forty-Five's">**The wardrobe.**</span> In stories, a way into another world. Halbert Quy's has coats in it, and behind the coats more coats, and then the back. *If you want another world, the back door is through there. It is the garden.*

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: the road north">**The wayshrine.**</span> A roof, a bench, and a box of supplies: FOR CHOSEN ONES. PLEASE REPLACE WHAT YOU TAKE. NOBODY EVER REPLACES WHAT THEY TAKE. In the book, forty-seven names, the last of them *Feeling good about this one!* The Forty-Eighth added his name and no comment.

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: Thistlewick">**The well that is not a wishing well.**</span> Says so. Is wished at anyway. Nothing happens. The sign was right.

<span class="trope" title="Walked into: a cave above the water">**The wrong dragon.**</span> A cave, <a href="../People/Somebody_Large">something very large</a> inside, breathing slowly, the way people breathe when they are reading. *Come in, come in. Mind the books.* Not in the Bestiary; it was not asked, and I am told it would rather be in the library.

<div class="marginalia">I asked the Forty-Eighth which of these had annoyed him most. He thought about it for some time, and said the barrels, and then said no, the barrels were fine, it was the pot that said INSIDE OF POT. I asked him why. He said that he had looked. - H.B.</div>
