# Arms and Artefacts

<p class="lede">The objects of the Obligatory Quest: swords above fireplaces, hats required by forms, benches in eleven pieces, and one crate, stencilled on all six faces, which you should not have opened.</p>

<p class="hatnote">The descriptions are mine. The column headed <b>Provenance</b> is Nix's, from her ledger, by her leave, and in her words, and she has asked me to say that where an object never passed through the back room she has entered it anyway, for completeness, and marked it so. Where she says <i>the party of the second part</i> she means the client, and the client has been identified, and it does not make any difference to the paperwork. For her, see <a href="../People/Nix">Nix</a>. - H.B.</p>

## Arms

| Object | What it is | Provenance (N.) |
|---|---|---|
| **The Village Sword** | The sword over the fireplace in Bram Thistle's house, which came off the wall when he was chosen with a sound that can only be described as smug. It had waited, Thistlewick says, a century. There is a clean rectangle of wall where it used to be. | Not located. Taken down, by the owner, off his own wall. Entered for completeness. |
| **<a href="The_Sword_of_Ultimate_Ending">The Sword of Ultimate Ending</a>** | A sword in a window at Penultima, behind a rope, at 9,999,999 crowns. | Not located. Not on any list. Some things are better in a window. |
| **The Star-Iron Sword** | One sword, out of the <a href="The_Sky_Anvil">Sky Anvil</a>, by Hob Grumnir. Takes the light like frost on a window. He has not said who it is for. | One stone, sky. Traded, not located. Held overnight. Returned. |
| **Beatrice** | Hob Grumnir's hammer, which he did not make. Disqualified at the Assize of Hammers under clause one, and right about everything. | Not located. Not entered. She would know. |
| **The Drawn Sword** | Out of a stone under Carn Drey, in an afternoon, with help. Roland Fairweather wears it. It has had one afternoon, and would like another. | Drawn, not located. From a stone, by R. Fairweather, with help. Witnessed. |

## Things for the road

| Object | What it is | Provenance (N.) |
|---|---|---|
| **A Hat** | Form A-1 requires a hat, and does not say which, so Mrs Barrow sells this one, for thirty. | Bought. Mrs Barrow. Not related. |
| **A Stick** | It is dangerous to go alone. Old Hollis at Firstfield has given eleven thousand, two hundred and six. One came back. | Given; returned; exchanged for a straighter one. The first is on a mantelpiece. |
| **A Bench (Flat-Packed)** | One bench, in eleven pieces, with a mallet and a diagram that is wrong. For the Forty-Fourth, who wanted somewhere to sit. | Carried out to the Barrow, built, facing the sea. Not located: commissioned. |
| **Lamp Oil** | One gallon, for the Lighthouse of Saint Bother, sold by Mrs Barrow, who marks it up. | Bought, at the mark-up. I would not have paid it. I was not asked. |
| **The Keeper's Lamp** | A small lamp off a large lighthouse. It is what the ones who come back walk home by. | Given, at the light. |
| **Forty Bolts of Oilskin** | Forty. Mrs Barrow counted them twice, out loud, and made somebody count them again after her. | Bought on account, Nether Sopping, for the Hoyle Works. Counted three times. |
| **The Lamp (Borrowed)** | Off the hook by the Door into the Mountain at Hithermost. Lent to the Forty-Eighth by Sir Dunstable, who had borrowed it. *Back directly.* | Lent, by Sir D., who borrowed it. On loan. Not entered as returned. Not entered as anything else either. |
| **A Bag, Packed** | Spare Cotterill's. Packed two years before the Quest. Contents: yes. | Not located. Packed, by its owner, at seven, and kept by the door. |
| **A Washer, Off Shim** | Shim Cotterill's second-best. Flat the whole time, which is why it is a good one. Worn on a bootlace. | Given, by Shim, to Spare. A good washer. Not the bearing. |
