# Afterwards: what the Thistle-pedia says happened next

The game stops at the morning after (`postgame.hand_back`) plus what the
post-game adds. Hosea writes later than that, so his bios have to say what
became of everybody, and it has to be the same story on every page. This is
that story, decided once. **Check a bio against it before writing one, and
add a line here in the same commit as anything new.**

Two kinds of line are below, and they are kept apart on purpose:

* **Canon** is something the game says: the tally (`journey.tally`), the
  companion lines (`postgame.CHAINS`), the post-game's pages, `TIMELINE.md`.
  The wiki may never contradict it.
* **Wiki** is ours: what happened between the game's last word and Hosea's
  desk. It is invented, and it must give way to the game. If the game ever
  says otherwise, the game wins and this file changes.

Where the player chose (who climbed the stair, which edition of Piper's
account exists, whether Bram had the cup), the wiki is vague, the way
`Index.md` is: everybody says it was them.

## When Hosea is writing

**The twenty-first of March, Third Age 2002**: "the first day of spring, which
Spare Cotterill has confirmed by letter". That is two years after the Quest
and about eighteen months after clause nineteen was struck, so the dust has
settled and lives have moved a little, but nobody is old yet. It is the date
on the front page's signature. Every "now" in the wiki means this date, and
every age is an age in 2002: add two to `TIMELINE.md` section 5.

## Who is where in March 2002

Canon first, then what the wiki adds. Ages are 2002's.

**Bram Thistle** (#48). *Canon:* home, turnips; holds the Receipt; the note
in clause twelve's place reads SEE BRAM and "nobody has needed to yet"; has
Fitch's key. *Wiki:* still in his own house, farming; still nobody has needed
to see him, though the Committee has twice minuted that they might. Uses
Fitch's cottage when he comes south, which is about monthly, to bring Hosea
corrections. Waters the dead thing in the pot each visit; nothing has grown;
Hosea checks. Walks up to the tower once a year, on the day, and the kettle is
on. Has declined a seat on the Committee because it meets in planting time.

**Grimspite** (formerly the Inevitable). *Canon:* retired; stays in the tower
in his chair with a kettle and the book at the bookmark; "Halbert said no as
well"; the forty-eighth carving will not be needed. *Wiki:* receives visitors
and gets about one a month, mostly people who want to see the chair. Writes to
Hosea in a beautiful hand, correcting dates. Has declined to say how old he
is (`TIMELINE.md` 6 keeps it undecided; so does he). Is still on the same book.

**Merribell**. *Canon:* the Order of Whatever Works won the Sisterhouse
nineteen to one; certified the party fit to travel; to speak at the Healers'
Table "next year". *Wiki:* spoke in 2001, for eleven minutes, the shortest
address in the Table's history and minuted as the most useful; the subject was
water. Still Thistlewick's healer, still the only member of her order for
eleven miles, now twenty-eight.

**Hob Grumnir**. *Canon:* the star-iron made one sword and he "has not said
who it is for"; the churchyard has a new gate; the Two Hundred's tube plate
was made by four hands; he and Bryd argue about rivets. *Wiki:* he still has
not said. Hosea has a theory and is not allowed to print it. Hob and Bryd sat
as joint judges of the Smiths' Hall prize in 2001 and disagreed for nine
hours; the prize was awarded, and neither will say to whom (NORTH.md 1.6:
rivals, nothing more).

**Zephyrine Vance**. *Canon:* the proxy vote, unused, "saving it"; the fines
paid; the Cinder Fen burnt, sanctioned; Dr Hapgood's name on his paper.
*Wiki:* still in Thistlewick, now on purpose; still has not used the vote; no
longer drinks at the Gilded Turnip like somebody waiting for a letter, because
the letter came.

**Nix** (she). *Canon:* the client identified and the coat fits; a RETURNED
column with eleven lines; Lorrimer plays the Dark Lord in Nether Sopping,
afternoons, and loses. *Wiki:* the back room is open; the ledger is into its
second volume; the parcel for a client not yet identified is still on the
shelf, and she will not say whose it is going to be.

**Sir Aldric Pemberton-Gore III**. *Canon:* the vigil kept; promoted Knight
Errant, At Large; has a squire who finds the gates; Duchess a partner in
haulage; reads the Odes to the last wild dragon. *Wiki:* found Thistlewick's
north gate unaided once, on the ninth of June 2001, and has not been able to
find it since; Hosea has the date from three witnesses.

**Piper Quill**. *Canon:* second at the Bell, by one point, to a thrush; the
ballad finished; the struck verses read and never printed; and an official
account that is either Ambrosine's, with a wolf, or A. and P. Quill's, with a
goat. *Wiki:* vague on the edition (a player choice: "there are, depending on
whom you ask, one official account or two"). Still writing it down. Hosea's
colleague and rival.

**Corvin Ash**. *Canon:* spoke at the Flood Dinner; wears the scarf in the
wrong colour; second on the Sable Hart's table, behind a scar; "He is up."
*Wiki:* asked back to the Flood Dinner of 2001, and went, and was early.
Thirty-two.

**Wren Halloway**. *Canon:* the monograph shelved at the Collegium, three
reviewers, one a duck; page four hundred says goose; the second plaque on the
goose. *Wiki:* welcome in the Slain Wyvern; sits under the goose, by choice.
Working on volume two, with a corrigendum about one specimen she will not name.

**Roland Fairweather**. *Canon:* his prophecy discharged under Carn Drey, with
help; rehired after the ending; "engaged again, between engagements"; Maud,
sixteen then, went to see one for herself. *Wiki:* has since finished two
further things and keeps a list. Is still asked to leave at doors, and now
mostly does not.

**Spare Cotterill** (eleven). *Canon:* home for Washer; still writing to
Effie; out of the lock with somebody there. *Wiki:* has written Hosea
thirty-one letters, all untrue, which Hosea files under Fiction and treasures;
it was Spare who confirmed the date of spring.

**Hosea Bellwether**. *Canon:* the history complete, the last page left blank
until Bram came back. *Wiki:* the last page of Volume Two is filled; Volume
Three is this wiki.

**Others.** *Halbert Quy* (#45): has heard all of it, tea included, and still
says no to a cup, but sends his regards up the hill (wiki). *Prosper
Hollowell*: served his notice (canon); stays on at the Former Premises,
unpaid, "pending instructions" (wiki). *Ott Hoyle*: the Two Hundred landed
adjacent (canon); the Two Hundred and First is on the slip (wiki). *Maud
Penhallow* (eighteen): either saw the end at the door and went to tell him,
or was told it over a pie and went to see one for herself (canon, a player
choice, so vague); is in the yard at Great Dunnage still, between (wiki).
