# Afterwards: what the Thistle-pedia says happened next

The game stops at the morning after (`postgame.hand_back`) plus what the
post-game adds. Hosea writes later than that, so his bios have to say what
became of everybody, and it has to be the same story on every page. This is
that story, decided once. **Check a bio against it before writing one, and
add a line here in the same commit as anything new.**

Two kinds of line are below, and they are kept apart on purpose:

* **Canon** is something the game says: the tally (`journey.tally`), the
  companion lines (`postgame.CHAINS`), the post-game's pages, `TIMELINE.md`.
  The wiki may never contradict it.
* **Wiki** is ours: what happened between the game's last word and Hosea's
  desk. It is invented, and it must give way to the game. If the game ever
  says otherwise, the game wins and this file changes.

Where the player chose (who climbed the stair, which edition of Piper's
account exists, whether Bram had the cup), the wiki is vague, the way
`Index.md` is: everybody says it was them.

## When Hosea is writing

**The twenty-first of March, Third Age 2002**: "the first day of spring, which
Spare Cotterill has confirmed by letter". That is two years after the Quest
and about eighteen months after clause nineteen was struck, so the dust has
settled and lives have moved a little, but nobody is old yet. It is the date
on the front page's signature. Every "now" in the wiki means this date, and
every age is an age in 2002: add two to `TIMELINE.md` section 5.

## Who is where in March 2002

Canon first, then what the wiki adds. Ages are 2002's.

**Bram Thistle** (#48). *Canon:* home, turnips; holds the Receipt; the note
in clause twelve's place reads SEE BRAM and "nobody has needed to yet"; has
Fitch's key. *Wiki:* still in his own house, farming; still nobody has needed
to see him, though the Committee has twice minuted that they might. Uses
Fitch's cottage when he comes south, which is about monthly, to bring Hosea
corrections. Waters the dead thing in the pot each visit; nothing has grown;
Hosea checks. Walks up to the tower once a year, on the day, and the kettle is
on. Has declined a seat on the Committee because it meets in planting time.

**Grimspite** (formerly the Inevitable). *Canon:* retired; stays in the tower
in his chair with a kettle and the book at the bookmark; "Halbert said no as

and gets about one a month, mostly people who want to see the chair. Writes to

**Merribell**. *Canon:* the Order of Whatever Works won the Sisterhouse
nineteen to one; certified the party fit to travel; to speak at the Healers'
Table "next year". *Wiki:* spoke in 2001, for eleven minutes, the shortest
address in the Table's history and minuted as the most useful; the subject was
water. Still Thistlewick's healer, still the only member of her order for
eleven miles, now twenty-eight.

**Hob Grumnir**. *Canon:* the star-iron made one sword and he "has not said
